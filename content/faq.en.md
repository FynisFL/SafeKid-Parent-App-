# Frequently asked questions — SafeKid Parent App

> Content by the DigiBabi team. This is the English version of `faq.vi.md`. If a question is not covered here, the app must say: "There is no information for this question yet. Please contact support."

## General

**What is SafeKid Parent App?**
An app for parents, running on your phone. It lets you see your phone's location on a map, set up safe zones, use the SOS button on this phone, manage tasks and rewards for your child, and keep device records.

**Do I need an account?**
No. You do not need to register, sign in, or enter an email or password.

**Does the app connect to my child's watch?**
No, not in this version. The app does not pair with a watch, does not receive the watch's location or battery, and does not receive SOS from a watch. Watch Passport is a manual record only.

**Whose location is shown on the map?**
The location of **the phone running the app** (the parent's phone). The app does not know your child's location.

**Where is my data stored?**
On this browser and device. The app has no server and does not sync data between devices or browsers. Export a backup regularly.

## Location and safe zones

**Why do I need to allow location access?**
So the app can read this phone's location and show it on the map. You can decline, but the map and safe zones will not work.

**Why is my location slow or inaccurate?**
GPS depends on your phone, the signal, and the surroundings. Indoors, underground, or between tall buildings, the location may be less accurate. The app shows the accuracy and the update time so you can judge it yourself.

**Do safe zones work when I close the app or lock the screen?**
No. Safe zones and reminders only work while the app is open in the browser. Keep the app open if you need monitoring.

**Why do I get "entered zone" or "left zone" alerts at odd times?**
GPS can fluctuate near a zone boundary. The app waits for several readings and uses a margin to reduce false alerts, but it cannot eliminate them completely.

## SOS

**What does the SOS button do?**
When you hold it long enough, the app saves an SOS event on this phone, records the location if available, and gives you options to call an emergency contact or open messages.

**Does SOS notify another phone or emergency services?**
**No.** SOS is only saved and notified on this device. The app has not sent any alert to other devices or emergency services.

**What should I do in an emergency?**
Call the emergency numbers right away: **113** (Police), **114** (Fire), **115** (Ambulance). You can also call a family member directly or contact the nearest authorities. Do not rely on the app for emergencies.

**Does the app call or send messages automatically?**
No. The app only opens the Phone or Messages app with prepared content. You must confirm and send in that app yourself.

**Does SOS work if I deny location or notification permission?**
Yes. The SOS event is still saved. If no location is available, the app says so.

## Tasks and rewards

**What are Coins?**
Coins are family reward points. They are not money, cannot be cashed out, and cannot be used to buy anything in this version.

**Can my child mark tasks as done?**
Not in this version, because the child has no app of their own. The parent marks tasks as done.

**If I mark a task done twice, do I get the reward twice?**
No. Each occurrence of a task gives its reward only once.

**Do task reminders work when I close the app?**
No. Reminders only appear while the app is open in the browser.

## Watch Passport

**What is Watch Passport?**
A place to keep device records that you enter yourself: purchase details, warranty, repair history, accessories, and attached documents.

**Does the app verify that the device is genuine?**
No. Everything in Watch Passport is entered by you and has not been verified by the manufacturer or seller. The app does not check serial numbers you enter.

**Does the data-wipe section erase data on the watch?**
No. It only records a checklist and evidence you enter. The app does not wipe any physical device.

**Does the app tell me when the warranty expires?**
Yes, based on the dates you entered. This is a local reminder, not a lookup with the manufacturer. Whether a warranty claim is accepted is up to the provider.

## Backup and data

**How do I back up my data?**
Go to **Profile → Data → Export backup**. The backup file contains your data and attached documents. Keep it somewhere safe.

**How do I move my data to another phone?**
Export a backup on the old device, then import it on the new one. Data does not sync automatically.

**Can I undo deleting app data?**
No. Export a backup before you delete.

**Can the browser delete the app's data?**
Yes, especially in Safari if you have not used the app for a long time. The app will remind you to back up regularly.

## S.F AI

**What is S.F AI?**
S.F AI is a built-in helper that answers questions about SafeKid and the frequently asked questions. It runs on this device and does not send your questions elsewhere.

**Can S.F AI handle emergencies or SOS?**
No. For emergencies, call 113, 114 or 115, or use the SOS screen.

**What if S.F AI cannot answer my question?**
It will say it is outside its scope and show a demo handoff notice. That notice does not send your request. Use the contact details below to reach our team.

## Language

**Can I change the language?**
Yes. Go to **Profile → Settings** and switch between Tiếng Việt and English.
