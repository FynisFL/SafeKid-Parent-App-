# Brand information — SafeKid (for S.F AI)

> Approved content. S.F AI may only answer within the entries below and the FAQ.

**What is SafeKid?**
SafeKid is a project by the DigiBabi team. The project's direction is a solution made of a smartwatch for children aged 3–10 and a management app for parents. It is currently at the design and prototype stage and has not been commercially released.

**Who is DigiBabi?**
DigiBabi is the team behind the SafeKid project.

**How is this app different from the SafeKid watch?**
This is a parent app that runs on its own on your phone. This version does not connect to a watch.

**What does SafeKid stand for?**
Three "3G" pillars: **Grow Safe** (safety: location, safe zones, SOS), **Grow Independent** (independence: habits, tasks, rewards) and **Grow Sustainable** (sustainability: device records and product lifecycle).

**What are Free and SafeKid Plus?**
This is a planned model and is not on sale. Core safety features such as SOS are not locked behind a paid plan. The planned Plus price is 55,000 VND/month or 449,000 VND/year; these are planned prices and cannot be purchased. This app has no payments.

**What is Core + Skin + Skill?**
A future direction for how the product could be customised. No working feature exists yet.

**What are Trade-in and Refurbished?**
Long-term directions for taking back and reusing devices. They are not implemented and no service is running.
